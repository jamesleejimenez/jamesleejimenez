# James Lee Jimenez

**AI operations and model evaluation.** I test large language models, find where they break, and write down how to fix it.

I work as an AI Training Specialist at Mercor, building evaluation environments for business and HR scenarios, writing the criteria models get graded against, and documenting why each run passed or failed. Before AI I spent five years running the operations side of small businesses, which is why I care whether a process survives contact with a busy team on a Friday afternoon.

Based in New Jersey. Open to NYC and remote.
